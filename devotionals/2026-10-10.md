# Acknowledge Him in All Our Ways — The Depth of God's Wisdom

**October 10, 2026**

**October 10, 2026 — Afternoon Devotional**

## Afternoon 🌄

### The Multifaceted Depth of Scripture

Many times we read and re-read scriptures and get new meanings, but even that understanding of scriptures being multifaceted like a diamond goes far beyond that statement.

When we think we understand something about scripture, then God shows us something we never saw before.

### Proverbs 3:5–6 — Acknowledge Him in All Our Ways

**Scripture — Proverbs 3:5–6 (NKJV)**

> Trust in the LORD with all your heart, And lean not on your own understanding; In all your ways acknowledge Him, And He shall direct your paths.

[Proverbs 3:5–6](https://bible.com/bible/114/PRO.3.5-6.NKJV)

Proverbs 3:5–6 is a perfect example.

As I was reading the verses, yes, we ask Him to guide us, but the scripture says to acknowledge Him in all our ways.

That also includes:

- "Yes God, you're welcome into my thoughts, my thought processes, what you have for me to do and also how to do it."
- "I thank you for being here with me."
- "I know you're working it out when I can't see you working."

"How do I do this?"

Acknowledgment also means to talk to and pray.

"Thank you for working this out."

He directs our paths when we acknowledge Him in all our ways.

### Proverbs 2:10–11 — Wisdom, Knowledge, and Discretion

**Scripture — Proverbs 2:10–11 (NKJV)**

> When wisdom enters your heart, And knowledge is pleasant to your soul, Discretion will preserve you; Understanding will keep you,

[Proverbs 2:10–11](https://bible.com/bible/114/PRO.2.10-11.NKJV)

When wisdom enters our heart, and knowledge is pleasant to our soul, discretion preserves me and understanding keeps me.

### Proverbs 2:12–15 — The Meaning of Frowardness

In verses 12–15, the word *froward* is more than just mere stubbornness or disobedience.

It describes a deeper moral situation.

In context, it's perversity, moral crookedness, distortion, and contrariness.

So discretion keeps us from and delivers us from the way of the evil man, from the man who speaks froward things.

**Scripture — Proverbs 2:12–15 (KJV)**

> To deliver thee from the way of the evil man, from the man that speaketh froward things;
>
> Who leave the paths of uprightness, to walk in the ways of darkness;
>
> Who rejoice to do evil, and delight in the frowardness of the wicked;
>
> Whose ways are crooked, and they froward in their paths:

[Proverbs 2:12–15](https://www.bible.com/bible/1/PRO.2.12-15.KJV)

### The Contrast Between Crooked Paths and God's Direction

This is a stark contrast to Proverbs 3:5, where God directs our paths when we acknowledge Him in all our ways.

## Summary

- Scriptures are multifaceted like a diamond, and God continues revealing things we have never seen before.
- Acknowledging God in all our ways includes welcoming Him into our thoughts, thought processes, decisions, and how we do things.
- Acknowledgment also means talking to God, praying, and thanking Him for working even when we cannot see Him working.
- Wisdom, knowledge, discretion, and understanding preserve and protect us from evil paths.
- The moral crookedness and frowardness described in Proverbs 2:12–15 stand in stark contrast to the paths God directs when we acknowledge Him.
