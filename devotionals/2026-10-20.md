# Trusting in the Lord, Prayer, Wisdom, and God's Peace

**October 20, 2026**

**Date:** October 20, 2026

## Morning 🌄

### Proverbs 3:1–8 — Trusting in the Lord

**Proverbs 3:1–2**

> My son, do not forget my law,
> But let your heart keep my commands;
> For length of days and long life
> And peace they will add to you.

[Proverbs 3:1–2 (NKJV)](https://www.bible.com/bible/114/PRO.3.1-2.NKJV)

Forget not my law but let my heart keep Your commandments. Doing this will add length of days and long life, and peace.

**Proverbs 3:3–4**

> Let not mercy and truth forsake you;
> Bind them around your neck,
> Write them on the tablet of your heart,
> And so find favor and high esteem
> In the sight of God and man.

[Proverbs 3:3–4 (NKJV)](https://www.bible.com/bible/114/PRO.3.3-4.NKJV)

Let not mercy and truth forsake me. Bind them around my neck. I write them on the tablets of my heart so I will find favor and good understanding in the sight of God and man.

**Proverbs 3:5–6**

> Trust in the LORD with all your heart,
> And lean not on your own understanding;
> In all your ways acknowledge Him,
> And He shall direct your paths.

[Proverbs 3:5–6 (NKJV)](https://www.bible.com/bible/114/PRO.3.5-6.NKJV)

Trust in the Lord with all of my heart. And I will lean not on my own understanding. In all my ways, I acknowledge You.

Show me what I need to do. I thank You for intervening, providing, giving me peace in my situation, and I thank You because of that You direct my paths.

### Philippians 4:6–7 — Prayer, Thanksgiving, and God's Peace

**Philippians 4:6–7**

> Be anxious for nothing, but in everything by prayer and supplication, with thanksgiving, let your requests be made known to God; and the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.

[Philippians 4:6–7 (NKJV)](https://www.bible.com/bible/114/PHP.4.6-7.NKJV)

And as You say in Philippians 4:6–7, "Be careful for nothing."

But in everything by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving, I let You know my requests.

I thank You that You've provided the finances, the people that will invest and participate in my dreams and business ideas.

I thank You, Lord, that You give me the wisdom in all situations, business interactions, people I should and shouldn't do business with, that is wisdom on that, and supernatural wealth and finances to fund these ideas and pursuits.

May I be blessed and holy unto You.

I thank You ahead of time.

In Jesus' name.

And amen, because I cast all my cares upon You.

### 1 Peter 5:6–7 — Humility and Casting My Cares on God

**1 Peter 5:7**

> casting all your care upon Him, for He cares for you.

[1 Peter 5:7 (NKJV)](https://www.bible.com/bible/114/1PE.5.7.NKJV)

The peace of God, which passes all understanding, shall keep my heart and mind through Christ Jesus.

**1 Peter 5:6**

> Therefore humble yourselves under the mighty hand of God, that He may exalt you in due time,

[1 Peter 5:6 (NKJV)](https://www.bible.com/bible/114/1PE.5.6.NKJV)

I humble myself under the mighty hand of God that He may exalt me in due time.

**1 Peter 5:7**

> casting all your care upon Him, for He cares for you.

[1 Peter 5:7 (NKJV)](https://www.bible.com/bible/114/1PE.5.7.NKJV)

I also cast all my cares upon Him because He cares for me.

### Proverbs 3:7–8 — Fearing the Lord and Departing from Evil

**Proverbs 3:7–8**

> Do not be wise in your own eyes;
> Fear the LORD and depart from evil.
> It will be health to your flesh,
> And strength to your bones.

[Proverbs 3:7–8 (NKJV)](https://www.bible.com/bible/114/PRO.3.7-8.NKJV)

I choose not to be wise in my understanding and in my own eyes.

But I fear the Lord and depart from evil.

It shall be health to my navel and marrow to my bones.

## Summary

- Keep God's commandments in my heart, receiving length of days, long life, and peace.
- Keep mercy and truth close, finding favor and good understanding in the sight of God and man.
- Trust in the Lord with all my heart, not leaning on my own understanding, and acknowledge Him in all my ways.
- Thank God for provision, finances, wisdom in business decisions, the right people, and supernatural wealth to fund my ideas and pursuits.
- Humble myself under God's mighty hand, cast all my cares upon Him, and trust His peace to guard my heart and mind through Christ Jesus.
