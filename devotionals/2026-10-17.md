# Faith, Fellowship, and Prayer

**October 17, 2026**

**October 17, 2026 — Evening Devotional**

## Evening 🌄

### Hebrews 11:6 — Without Faith It Is Impossible to Please God

> But without faith it is impossible to please him: for he that cometh to God must believe that he is, and that he is a rewarder of them that diligently seek him.

[Hebrews 11:6 KJV](https://www.bible.com/bible/1/HEB.11.6.KJV)

### Don't Forget — Hebrews 10:25

Don't forget.

> Not forsaking the assembling of ourselves together, as the manner of some is; but exhorting one another: and so much the more, as ye see the day approaching.

[Hebrews 10:25 KJV](https://www.bible.com/bible/1/HEB.10.25.KJV)

### Philippians 4:6–7 — Prayer, Thanksgiving, and the Peace of God

> Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God.
>
> And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.

[Philippians 4:6–7 KJV](https://www.bible.com/bible/1/PHP.4.6-7.KJV)

## Summary

- Without faith, it is impossible to please God.
- Those who come to God must believe that He is and that He rewards those who diligently seek Him.
- Do not forsake assembling together; exhort one another, especially as the day approaches.
- In everything, bring requests to God through prayer and supplication with thanksgiving.
- God's peace, which surpasses all understanding, will keep our hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.
