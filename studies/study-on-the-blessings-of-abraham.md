## Notes

### Forget Not All His Benefits

[Psalm 103](https://bible.com/bible/114/PSA.103.NKJV)

Forget not ALL His benefits:

1. Forgives all iniquities — v. 3
2. Heals all our diseases — v. 3
3. Redeems our life from destruction — v. 4
4. Crowns us with lovingkindness and tender mercies — v. 4
5. Satisfies our mouths with good things, so we're renewed like the eagle — v. 5
6. Executes righteousness and justice for all who are oppressed — v. 6
7. Made His ways known through His acts with Moses and the Israelites; demonstrated — v. 7
8. The Lord is merciful and gracious, slow to anger, and abounding in mercy towards us — v. 8
9. Doesn't strive with us forever or keep His anger — v. 9
10. Doesn't deal with us the way we should be dealt with, and doesn't punish us according to our iniquities — v. 10
11. His mercy is as high as the heavens are above the earth toward those who fear Him — v. 11
12. He removes our transgressions from us as far as the east is from the west — v. 12
13. He pities us as a father pities his children — v. 13
14. He knows and remembers our frame and understands our composition — v. 14
15. His mercy is from everlasting to everlasting — v. 17

## Summary
- God promised Abraham that He would make him a great nation and that he would be a blessing.
- The Lord blessed Abraham in all things.
- Abraham lived to a good old age and was full of years.
- Psalm 103 records God's benefits, including forgiveness, healing, redemption, lovingkindness, mercy, righteousness, and justice.
- Jesus redeemed us from the curse of the law so that the blessing of Abraham might come upon the Gentiles in Christ Jesus through faith.
