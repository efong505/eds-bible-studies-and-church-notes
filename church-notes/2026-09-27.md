# Sunday, September 27th Service, Pastor Hank

**September 27, 2026**

## Offering

### Deuteronomy 16:13-15

Observe.

Businesses make money. At the time of the harvest, He'll bless us in all our increase.

All the work of your hands, then shalt surely rejoice.

> “You shall observe the Feast of Tabernacles seven days, when you have gathered from your threshing floor and from your winepress. And you shall rejoice in your feast, you and your son and your daughter, your male servant and your female servant and the Levite, the stranger and the fatherless and the widow, who are within your gates. Seven days you shall keep a sacred feast to the LORD your God in the place which the LORD chooses, because the LORD your God will bless you in all your produce and in all the work of your hands, so that you surely rejoice.”
>
> — Deuteronomy 16:13-15 NKJV

[Deuteronomy 16:13-15](https://bible.com/bible/114/DEU.16.13-15.NKJV)

## Message

### Pray and Don't Give Up

> “Then He spoke a parable to them, that men always ought to pray and not lose heart.”
>
> — Luke 18:1 NKJV

[Luke 18:1](https://bible.com/bible/114/LUK.18.1.NKJV)

When it really is, when the frogs showed up, don't be surprised and don't vote.

The frogs are symbolic of demonic.

Pharaoh said, “Let there be one more day with frogs.”

People should pray often and not faint.

Will the Son of Man find faith when He comes back?

> “Nevertheless, when the Son of Man comes, will He really find faith on the earth?”
>
> — Luke 18:8 NKJV

[Luke 18:8](https://bible.com/bible/114/LUK.18.8.NKJV)

### Don't Put Up With the Frogs

God is using light and sound as He prepares us.

Don't put up with the frogs.

Take the spoils. Don't repeat.

### Do Not Let Fear Rule

We need to get our head straight.

> “men’s hearts failing them from fear and the expectation of those things which are coming on the earth, for the powers of the heavens will be shaken.”
>
> — Luke 21:26 NKJV

[Luke 21:26](https://bible.com/bible/114/LUK.21.26.NKJV)

Men's hearts failing because of fear, looking at the wrong thing.

## Summary
- God blesses our produce and the work of our hands so that we may rejoice.
- Pray often and do not faint or give up.
- Remain in faith as we wait for the Son of Man.
- Don't put up with the frogs.
- Do not allow fear to control where you look or what you focus on.
