## Message

### Page 1

**Luke 18:1**

Jesus set priority for us to pray always and not to faint.

[Luke 18:1 NKJV](https://bible.com/bible/114/luk.18.1.NKJV)

What should we do when we see fearful sights? Up our prayer.

Need to pray.

**vs. 2–5**

The unjust judge didn't answer but knew that if he didn't respond, it would wear him out.

[Luke 18:2–5 NKJV](https://bible.com/bible/114/luk.18.2-5.NKJV)

**vs. 6–8**

We are getting set up by the Lord to be avenged.

God will avenge speedily. There is an acceleration.

Take the moments to pray or to be in a place of vengeance for the things of darkness.

People get tired because they come to a place of fighting.

Get out there and keep doing it.

**Judges 8**

Feed on things that are victories.

Ignore the propaganda.

Don't magnify it!

### Page 2

Feed on the promise! Promise.

**Judges 8:4**

Don't sit there and wait.

[Judges 8:4 NKJV](https://bible.com/bible/114/jdg.8.4.NKJV)

Focus on what He said.

"This nation is going forward regardless."

**Isaiah 43:18**

Remember not your past.

[Isaiah 43:18 NKJV](https://bible.com/bible/114/isa.43.18.NKJV)

**Numbers 14:1–3**

They forgot their own previous victories instead of what God said.

[Numbers 14:1–3 NKJV](https://bible.com/bible/114/num.14.1-3.NKJV)

They murmered against President Moses and Vice President Vance (Aaron).

Then they said "I'm a newer Moses." And then said "No Kings."

The people murmured wanting to go back to the nonsense that happened with the Biden era, Obama era.

**vs. 3**

"We dedicated our Nation to God, now looks at the gas prices."

And we still have our cities! Israel is still around!

### Page 3

They see and complain about how things are falling apart, the problems, the struggles of man in human spirits.

I looked at a judge, "You're out!"

They forgot about how they treated your children.

Democratic Socialists want to bring in Islamic extremism, to bring in Islam where a woman has no rights.

The wives and children are always the victims.

They wanted to go back to this nonsense.

Christian Nationalism was devised to silence Christians, to attempt to not have Christians involved in politics and not fight against religious liberty attacks.

When you got 95% of news not reporting what really is happening then you really don't know what victories are really happening.

### Page 4

Don't know what victories are really happening.

**vs. 4**

"Let's get a new president," "Let's return to the good ole Biden days."

Nonsense!!

God is a just God not a politician God.

Get out and vote. 22% of Christians vote. 82% that are registered to vote sit out.

**vs. 5**

We need to set our faces and eyes on God.

We are not going back.
